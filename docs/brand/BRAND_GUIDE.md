# AdvisorTrack Brand Guide

## Colours

Primary Blue: #0E51E4  
Dark Navy: #020921  
Border Grey: #E9E9E9  
White: #FFFFFF

### Text on Light

Heading: #020921  
Body: #020921

### Text on Dark

Heading: #FFFFFF  
Body: #FFFFFF

## Typography

Headings: Montserrat 800  
Body: Montserrat 400  
Buttons: Montserrat 600

## Components

Card Radius: 10px  
Card Shadow: None  
Button Radius: 5px  
Button Hover: retain colour with a subtle scale-down interaction

## Brand Direction

- Modern premium SaaS / financial-services technology
- Strong dark navy and electric-blue identity
- High contrast
- Clean and minimal
- Professional enterprise appearance
- Avoid unnecessary decorative effects
- Dashboard remains primarily light, with the dark navy/blue branding carried through the sidebar, controls, typography and selected accents

## Usage

- These are the approved AdvisorTrack brand specifications supplied by Mike.
- The assets under `docs/brand/source/` are the untouched source assets, with their original filenames and formats preserved.
- The assets under `public/brand/` are web-ready copies for the Dashboard. They must not be treated as replacements for the approved source package.
- Future Dashboard visual work should use this guide as the source of truth.
- AdvisorTrack branding must not be guessed from the old or live AdvisorTrack website.
- `docs/brand/source/AdvisorTrack Logo Final/Homepage Screenshot.png` is retained as an approved visual-direction reference.

No font files are included in this package. Montserrat is documented here as the approved typeface.
